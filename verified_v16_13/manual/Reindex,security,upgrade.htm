MOH fee schedule updates

MOH Fees Update accepts the official OHIP Physician Fee Schedule Master as a ZIP, text, or .001 file. It can use the newest matching file in Downloads, let you choose a file, or open the official Ontario download page. The source download is retained and a dated backup copy is made.
