MOSt V16.68.2 / 1.7.705 - XP WORKSTATION TEST

STATUS
This is a test release, not an approved clinic-wide deployment. It combines the
current V16.66 scheduler, printer, label, Help and report-performance source with
the current consultation, Claims-session, diagram and label source. V16.68.2 recognizes that LetterBuilder
closes the original chart before opening the consultation, leaving Word's
document count at one. It identifies the replacement document by its different
full name before looking for the exact `[[CONSULT]]` insertion marker. It
searches the merged letter's body, headers, footers, linked stories and text
boxes. Its messages are attached to Word, and the merged letter is reactivated.
It performs no database upgrade and must not be installed on the server.

SAFE INSTALLATION
1. Make a normal verified server/data backup.
2. Begin with one Windows XP workstation only.
3. Close MOSt on that workstation. For the first launch, close MOSt on every
   other workstation as an added safety check.
4. Extract the complete ZIP into one folder.
5. Run Install-MOSt-V16.68.2-XP.cmd as a local Administrator.
6. Keep the Upgrade_Backup_V16_68_2 folder reported by the installer.
7. Start MOSt and verify that the displayed executable is 1.7.705.

CONSULTATION TEST
1. Open LetterBuilder and leave it open for the entire test.
2. Put `[[CONSULT]]` exactly once in the consultation Word template, including
   both pairs of square brackets. Open one test patient's chart in Word. It
   must be the only Word document open and contain a Vision or Vision: heading.
3. Click Consult in LetterBuilder. Confirm that MOSt reports the information is
   prepared.
4. Click the existing New button, choose the normal consultation template, and
   verify or correct the referring source.
5. Click the existing Word button. Do not return to LetterBuilder.
6. When the merged document opens, confirm that the insertion question appears
   over the workflow. Press Enter (Yes).
7. Confirm that text beginning at the chart's final Vision: replaces
   `[[CONSULT]]` in the merged consultation and the patient chart is unchanged.
8. Use the normal existing consultation save/file process. The new code must
   not save, rename, close or file the document automatically.
9. Repeat once after cancelling the insertion question. Neither document should
   be changed by the cancelled insertion.

KNOWN CONSULTATION LIMITATION
The V16.67.8 Hide workaround was removed because it closed Word and
LetterBuilder. Consultation insertion should work and both windows must remain
open; Word foreground behavior remains under investigation.

CLAIMS SESSION BILLING MD TEST
1. Open Claims for a patient and select the Billing MD.
2. Save a claim, then press Claims again from the Patient form. Confirm the same
   Billing MD remains selected.
3. Repeat through Letters + Claims and with another patient during the same
   open Claims-form session. The Billing MD must remain selected.
4. Close Claims completely, reopen it, and confirm Billing MD starts blank and
   must be selected for the first billing of the new session.
5. Enter a normal service and add a premium from the bubble. Choose its time
   and code. Confirm the proper premium fee appears immediately, not $0.00.
6. Save the claim. Confirm every old claim-entry row clears for the next claim
   without closing Claims, while the session Billing MD remains selected.

DIAGRAM TEST
1. Confirm upper-left and upper-right black deviation boxes are present near
   the centre positions, not at the outside edges.
2. In a crowded position enter `LXT5`, press Enter, then enter `RH3`.
3. Confirm the entry box displays two lines and the copied/inserted diagram
   displays both lines distinctly and inside the diagram boundary.

LAYOUT AND REGRESSION CHECK
- Confirm the row reads Consult, Diagram, Letters, Exit without overlap and is
  shifted left with less empty space after Exit.
- Close and reopen/raise LetterBuilder several times; the buttons must not drift.
- Recheck Scheduler search/new appointments, Patients, Claims, EDT, billing,
  Help, Daily Summary, Check In, patient-label printing and the eye diagram.
- Print an Epson TM-U220 patient label. Confirm the five retained lines are
  compact and single-spaced, and Printed and Last Edit do not appear.

ROLLBACK
Close MOSt. Restore the files from the installer-created
Upgrade_Backup_V16_68_2 folder to the same installation locations. Restore the
two label files to the labels subfolder. Do not alter any database files.

Report the exact message and step if the consultation prompt does not appear,
the marker is not found, the wrong Word document changes, or the button row
overlaps.
