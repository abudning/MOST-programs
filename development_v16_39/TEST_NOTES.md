# V16.39 XP LetterBuilder details and sphere-only glasses import

Executable: **1.7.640**

- LetterBuilder performs a full form update, directly builds the AB existing-letter list, selects the first item, and invokes its Click routine to populate template, MD, details, and action buttons.
- Glasses import accepts full sphere/cylinder/axis lines with arbitrary spaces or tabs, and sphere-only lines such as `OD: +0.50` and `OS: +0.50`. Sphere-only cylinder and axis default to zero.
- Window placement and manual-position preservation are unchanged.
