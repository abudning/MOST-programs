# MOSt V16.64 Third Party Billing development

V16.64 is the development line after the preserved V16.63 WORKING WELL
release. The first scaffold adds a five-line free-hand Third Party Billing
form and a `thirdparty_lines` detail table. The existing Guarantor table and
payment/history code remain compatible while the new workflow is integrated.

The next steps are to connect the Patients Third Party button, save a bill as
a normal Third Party claim, add the revised letterhead report, and connect
Accept Payment and history to the new detail lines.
