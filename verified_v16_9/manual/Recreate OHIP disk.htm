Recreate OHIP disk: Allows you to recreate your OHIP disk in the event that it is damaged between your office and the OHIP computers.

 

Method:

Choose the submitted file to be recreated

by clicking on it with the left mouse button.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

The file will highlight in blue.

 

 

Move your mouse: over the button between the boxes, and copy to floppy will appear as bubble help.

** Ensure a floppy disk is in your drive

Click on the button: Copy to floppy

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

The file will be copied: to the floppy disk

 

Click: Exit to end.

 

Remove your floppy and submit to OHIP.

Current submission recreation

Recreate OHIP Submission now uses the configured MOSt backup and output folders. EDT files are restored to the EDT\OUT folder; non-EDT files are restored to the OHIP\OUT folder. A floppy disk is no longer required.
